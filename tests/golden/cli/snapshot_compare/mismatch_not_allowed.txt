Limitations:
  - This comparison reports what differs between two calibration snapshots of one backend, for the physical resources of one run's footprint. Deltas are candidate minus baseline.
  - The footprint run only selects which physical resources are read. The run is not compared with either snapshot.
  - Values are provider-reported and compared exactly, with no tolerance. A parameter whose unit differs between the snapshots is not compared, and no unit is converted.
  - Relevant hardware means calibration data for the physical resources of the footprint. It does not mean a parameter is known to affect workload performance.
  - An unchanged value that was not re-measured (the same measurement date in both snapshots) is one measurement reported twice. It is not evidence that the parameter held constant between the captures.
  - A changed measurement date is the provider's timestamp. For virtual gates (such as rz) and configured durations (such as gate_length), the provider can update the date without a physical measurement, so 're-measured' may not mean physically re-measured.
  - Error-type parameters with a value of exactly 1 are listed as the provider reported them. QCI does not interpret them.
  - Pairwise general parameters (D14) and measure gate entries (D22) are not compared.
  - Snapshot comparisons are never stored.
