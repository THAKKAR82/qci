Limitations:
  - This comparison reports what differs between the calibration frozen into a fake backend, as stored in one run, and one live snapshot of the device the fake was built from, for the physical resources of that run's footprint. Deltas and date differences are live minus fake.
  - The fake-to-device correspondence comes from an explicit list in the provider adapter and is checked against the device name recorded in the fake's own calibration payload. QCI cannot check that the fake's payload equals what the device published at that time.
  - Both sides are reports about one device at different times. Qubit and gate indices are assumed to name the same physical resources on both sides. A device revision between the two dates (both backend versions are shown) could break this, and QCI cannot detect it.
  - Values are provider-reported and compared exactly, with no tolerance. A parameter whose unit differs between the two sides is not compared, and no unit is converted.
  - A changed value means only that the two reports give different values. This comparison does not say whether the fake's noise model suits the workload, and it does not predict how the workload would behave on the device. Nothing is simulated against the live snapshot.
  - This compares calibration data: the values the fake backend was built from and the live device's reported values. It does not compare the noise model a simulator constructs from that data; a simulator may use only some of these parameters, in its own way.
  - Measurement dates are provider timestamps, shown as given. Their differences are calculated. For virtual gates (such as rz) and configured durations (such as gate_length), the date can be the document timestamp rather than the time of a physical measurement.
  - The run's captured_at is when QCI read the fake backend, not a calibration date, and is not used.
  - Relevant hardware means calibration data for the physical resources of the footprint. It does not mean a parameter is known to affect workload performance.
  - The raw payloads of a fake and a live device always differ, so global_snapshot_changed is true in practice and carries no further information here.
  - Error-type parameters with a value of exactly 1 are listed as the provider reported them. QCI does not interpret them.
  - Pairwise general parameters (D14) and measure gate entries (D22) are not compared.
  - These comparisons are never stored.
